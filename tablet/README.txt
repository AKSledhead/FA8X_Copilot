FA8X Copilot - the EFB

The EFB model is the "Generic Tablet" by BK (RandomUser), 2024, from
https://forums.x-plane.org/files/file/97877-generic-tablet-object-for-avitab-or-a-custom-efb/
licensed under the EUPL v1.2 (https://eupl.eu/1.2/en/). Changed for the FA8X Copilot: placed on the
Falcon 8X's captain side ledge, a stowed copy in the pocket by the seat, the plugin's own screen and
controls in place of AviTab and xlua.

fa8x_tablet.obj          the EFB, its mount and the stowed copy
fa8x_tablet_cockpit.obj  its click zones and screen (merged into the Falcon's cockpit object)
fa8x_tablet*.dds/.png    its textures

The plugin adds these to the Falcon itself when Settings > EFB is on, and takes them out again when
it's off.
